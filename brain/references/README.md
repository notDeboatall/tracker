# Visual references

These two screenshots are **mood references, not templates to copy**. Take the specific ideas listed; leave the rest.

## ref-1-pastel-schedule-and-heatmap.png
**Take:**
- Soft pastel tinted cards for schedule items, with a round checkbox on the right.
- The month heatmap (rounded day circles filled by intensity): use this idea for the Tracker (21-day grid) and Week view.
- Bold, simple section headings ("Schedule", "Activity") with a small "See all" link.

**Leave:**
- The bright saturated pink/yellow/blue/green (too loud for a 10-block day; use the muted tints in `12_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`).
- The pet/health theme and photo avatars.

## ref-2-overview-tiles-and-center-plus.png
**Take:**
- The bottom bar with a big black "+" in the centre: this is our "add work" button.
- Overview tiles in tinted colours with a big number: use for the three streak tiles (Gym, DSA, AIML).
- The Today's Task list: a small type label above each title, time on the right, circle checkbox/icon on the left.
- The black pill selected-state (e.g. "My Project").

**Leave:**
- The lavender gradient background and blurred glass panels.
- The purple accent and the squiggly sparkline decorations (decorative, carry no meaning).
- The welcome-with-avatar header; ours is more personal (see `12_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`).
